# IT Enablement

## Definition / Formula

The core idea is that technology implementation must begin with business purpose, not with software or infrastructure. Decide the mission; next, choose the strategy to win; then define the moves; select the tools; and finally ensure the system can run them reliably

## Explanation

 The following sequence must be kept in mind (in the exact same order) when we are implementing technology in business

```
Purpose -> Theory of business -> Process -> Software -> Infrastructure
```

> Theory of business is the belief about how a company’s actions will create value for customers and achieve business goals

For example, if a company wants to reduce customer delivery delays, its purpose is faster, more reliable delivery. Its theory of business might be that better demand forecasting and route planning will improve delivery performance. Its process would define how orders are prioritized, routes are assigned, and delays are handled. Only then should it select logistics software and build the required cloud infrastructure, databases, and networks.
