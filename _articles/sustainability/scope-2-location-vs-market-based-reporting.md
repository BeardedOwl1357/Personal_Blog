# Scope 2 location vs market based reporting

## Definition / Formula

A factory needs power. They get this from local energy grid. This grid gets powers from various electricity generation centers. The way it reports its emissions differs based on commitment to renewable energy

## Explanation

A factory needs power. They get this from local energy grid. This grid gets powers from various electricity generation centers. For this example, let's say
1. A thermal power plant that uses coal (70%)
2. A solar farm (30%)

Let us assume that using both the energy resources, emission is 0.5 per MWh and without solar, emission is 0.6 per MWh
- Location based : Use the full mix. 0.5 per MWh
- Market based : Take renewable as 0 and take remaining as 0.6

Location-based accounting asks, “What is the average emissions intensity of the grid supplying this facility?” Market-based accounting asks, “What emissions attributes (renewable and non renewable) are associated with the electricity the company has contractually chosen?” They should not be mixed up or treated as interchangeable.
