# Meeting people through shared activities

## Definition / Formula

Meeting people through shared activities requires a recurring, low-stakes, structured social channel that fits one's personality

## Explanation

A billiards league/regular table, a drawing class, a board-games meetup, a reading group. All of these interests can become activities through which one can meet people. The overwhelm with socializing in general ("too many steps to process") is real, and one of the fixes is to reduce it to _one_ step: pick one channel, commit to showing up twice, then decide. Not "figure out how to meet people" but "Show up at X on Tuesday".
