# Life stages and meaning

## Definition / Formula

Meaningful life isn’t one fixed thing. What matters most shifts with each decade, like a game that changes its rules at each level. In your 20s you **explore**, in your 30s you **commit**, in your 40s you **cut**, in your 50s you **teach**, in your 60s you **rediscover**, in your 70s you **connect**, and in your 80s you **pass things on**. Each stage has a theme and one small action.

## Explanation


| Decade  | Theme      | Core Idea                                                                                                                                                                  | Action                                                                               |
| ------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **20s** | Experience | Collect experiences before you collect obligations. Try jobs, places, relationships and interests to learn what kind of life you actually enjoy living.                    | Try something you've been curious about without asking whether it helps your career. |
| **30s** | Commit     | Meaning starts requiring choices. Time is limited across work, relationships, family, friends and yourself, so decide what gets genuine commitment.                        | Choose one relationship or priority that deserves more of your time this month.      |
| **40s** | Edit       | Success can leave you carrying goals you no longer care about. Some ambitions deserve another decade; others have been on the list too long.                               | Remove one goal you're pursuing mostly because of expectation, status or habit.      |
| **50s** | Share      | What you've learned becomes useful to someone behind you. Your mistakes, judgement and experience can save others years of figuring things out alone.                      | Give one younger person an hour of your time with no agenda attached.                |
| **60s** | Redefine   | Work may stop being the main answer to "what do you do?" That creates space to rediscover interests, relationships and parts of yourself your career couldn't accommodate. | Restart something you enjoyed before work became the centre of your week.            |
| **70s** | Belong     | The people around you matter enormously. Friendships and communities don't maintain themselves once work stops filling your calendar with people.                          | Create one recurring reason to see people you enjoy being around.                    |
| **80s** | Give       | Your attention, stories, experience and presence still matter to the people around you. Milestones should take a back seat.                                                | Pass something on: a lesson, tradition, skill or story worth keeping.                |
