# IT Interaction model

## Definition / Formula

Software carries its own assumptions about how work should be done. The theory of the business should shape the technology, not the other way round.

## Explanation

The effects of an information system come from the interaction of three things
- The system's features
- The organizational context (strategy, structure, culture, business processes, existing IT) 
- The implementation process. 

Processes must be defined down to the atomic step

"We provide great customer service" is a goal, not a process. A process is usable — and programmable — only when it is broken down level by level until each step is atomic: a single action with a clear actor, trigger, input, rule, output and time standard. A simple test: could a new employee perform the step with no further instruction? If not, keep going down.

Technology turns processes into software running on robustinfrastructure Implementation means converting those atomic steps into programs, data and rules. The programs then run on infrastructure — networks, servers, data centres, backups. That infrastructure must be designed for the worst day, not the average one: peak demand, outages, disasters and crises.
