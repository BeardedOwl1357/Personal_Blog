# Make vs Buy

## Definition / Formula

Build when strategic differentiation, customization, and control matter enough to justify the cost. Outsource when the activity is relatively standard and an external provider can deliver it more efficiently or effectively. Choose a hybrid model when the company needs external efficiency but must retain control over its core capabilities.

## Explanation

The 5 factors that drive the decision

1. `Strategic importance`: Companies usually build systems that give them a competitive advantage and outsource systems that are common across industries. For example, a bank may build a unique fraud detection system because it protects its business advantage, but outsource employee payroll because payroll processing is not what makes the bank special.
2. Cost and time: Outsourcing often reduces upfront investment and speeds up implementation, while building requires developers, infrastructure, and ongoing maintenance. For example, a startup may rent cloud services instead of buying servers because it can launch faster without spending heavily at the start. However, outsourcing can become more expensive over time through subscription fees and vendor charges.
3. Internal expertise: A company builds when it has the skills and resources to develop and maintain a system effectively, and outsources when an external provider has better expertise. For example, a retailer without cybersecurity specialists may hire a security firm rather than build its own security team from scratch.
4. Control and customization: Building gives a company greater control over features, data, upgrades, and integration with existing systems. Outsourcing is attractive when standard features are sufficient, but the company may become dependent on the vendor's pricing, roadmap, and service quality. Think of building as creating your own character in a game and outsourcing as choosing a ready-made character with limited customization.
5. Risk and flexibility: Building creates risks such as project delays, technical failures, and employee turnover, while outsourcing creates risks such as data breaches, vendor lock-in, and service disruptions. Companies must decide which risks they can manage better. For sensitive customer data or mission-critical systems, they may prefer stronger internal control, although a specialist vendor can sometimes provide better security.

### The hybrid approach

The best answer is not always build or outsource because companies can also use a hybrid approach. Consider an e-commerce company: it builds its recommendation algorithm because personalization drives sales, buys a standard HR system because HR administration is not its competitive advantage, and uses a cloud provider for infrastructure while retaining control over its application and customer experience.
