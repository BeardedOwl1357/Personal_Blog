# Theory of firm

## Definition / Formula

In "The Nature of the Firm" (1937), Coase asked why companies exist at all if markets are
efficient. His answer: using the market has costs

## Explanation

A firm brings work inside when doing so costs less than buying it each time. Using the market has costs such as finding suppliers, negotiating, writing contracts, checking quality, enforcing agreements.

Most transaction costs are really information costs: knowing who can do the job, at what price, and whether it was done well. So a firm is, at heart, a system for processing information.
