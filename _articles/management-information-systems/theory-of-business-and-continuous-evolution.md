# Theory of business and continuous evolution

## Definition / Formula

Every organization runs on a set of assumptions, whether written down or not. The environment changes, so the theory of the business must be retested, processes redesigned and software rewritten. The companies that benefit are the ones that treat this as a permanent capability, not a one-time project.

## Explanation

The set of assumptions over the business runs on
- Environment: who the customers are, what the market values, what technology and competitors are doing.

- Mission: what results the organization exists to produce.

- Core competencies: what it must be excellent at to deliver that mission.

A sound theory fits reality, its three parts fit each other, it is understood across the organization, and it is tested constantly. When reality changes and the theory does not, even well-run companies decline.

Since businesses environments and other variables change, any technology adoption should be considered as a lifetime project than a one-time thing
