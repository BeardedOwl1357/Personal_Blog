# Funding Sources: Equity vs. Liabilities

## Definition / Formula

Assets = Funding (Equity + Liabilities)

## Explanation

To acquire assets, a business needs capital or funding. This money can come from two distinct sources: the owners themselves (equity) or external parties (liabilities/borrowings).

If we borrow money from someone and have an obligation to repay it in the future, it is called a liability. If, instead of requiring repayment, the person provides money in exchange for a share in the profits, it is called equity. Equity represents the 'distribution of something as per contribution' and determines how much control a shareholder has. In a company, all assets must equal total funding (Assets = Equity + Liabilities) because any funding raised is used to acquire the assets of the firm.
