# Steps of accounting

## Definition / Formula

Accounting is the language that enables communication of financial activities of a business. There are some steps of processing financial information

## Explanation

Steps are
        - Identify transactions and events to be recorded
        - Measure the transactions : Monetary units
        - Record them : Journal
        - Classify them : Ledger
        - Summarise them : Trial balance, P&L statement, Balance sheet
        - Analysis and interpretation : Cash flow statement and Ratio analysis
        - Communicating : Report creation
