# Risk Management Assets (Current Assets)

## Definition / Formula

Current Assets (Short-term, <=12 Months)

## Explanation

Risk management assets are short-term resources held by a company to ensure smooth business operations and mitigate liquidity risks. These assets are expected to be consumed, sold, or converted into cash within a 12-month period.

On the Balance Sheet, they are classified as Current Assets. Typical examples include inventories (goods ready for sale), receivables (money owed by credit customers), short-term investments, and cash on hand. Having adequate current assets protects the business against sudden cash shortages.
