# Assets vs. Expenses

## Definition / Formula

Assets (Create money) vs. Expenses (Consume money)

## Explanation

It is critical for a business to distinguish between money spent to build future earning power and money that is consumed in current operations.

An asset is a resource used to generate money over time. An expense, on the other hand, is a cash outflow for items or services that are consumed immediately and do not generate future revenue. While assets build the business's capacity to earn, expenses represent the cost of running the business today.
