# Income Generating Assets (Non-Current Assets)

## Definition / Formula

Non-Current Assets (Long-term, >12 Months)

## Explanation

Income generating assets, often referred to as capital assets, are long-term resources used to produce goods and services. They provide economic benefits over multiple years and are reported on the Balance Sheet as Non-Current Assets.

These assets can be tangible or intangible. Tangible assets have a physical form, such as factories, machinery, equipment, and company vehicles. Intangible assets do not have a physical presence but hold immense economic value, such as patents, brand names, copyrights, and goodwill.
