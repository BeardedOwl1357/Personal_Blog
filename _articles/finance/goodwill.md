# Goodwill

## Definition / Formula

Goodwill = Fair Value (Purchase Price) - Book Value

## Explanation

Goodwill is an intangible asset that is created when one company acquires another. It represents the premium paid by the acquirer over the fair market value of the target company's identifiable net physical assets.

This extra price is paid because the target company possesses highly valuable, non-physical assets that are not fully captured on its balance sheet. These include loyal customer relationships, strong brand recognition, proprietary technologies, or employee expertise. Goodwill is reported as a non-current asset on the acquirer's balance sheet post-acquisition.
