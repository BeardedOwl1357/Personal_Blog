# Journal Entries

Source : [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Di-zoaiMlX4&list=PLaAhQ2ofZZRCJIUbTjeUXx4JoZ7CyGHr3&index=9](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Di-zoaiMlX4&list=PLaAhQ2ofZZRCJIUbTjeUXx4JoZ7CyGHr3&index=9)


## Definition / Formula

Accounting is a language with its own set of rules. Journal entrying is the step through which we convert normal everyday transactions into accounting transactions

## Explanation

There are primarily 3 types of accounts
- Personal : They represent a person / entity
- Real : They represent asset / property of a business. Particularly, something that is tangible and part of business
- Nominal : Generally covers intangible part of business like expense, loss, income, salary, purchase, gain etc. An abstract part of business

## Rule of Debit / Credit

| Type | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal | Recieved | Given |
| Real | Inflow | Outflow |
| Nominal | Expenses and losses | Income and gain |

Note : All the above is seen from a perspective of a business. If someone gives money to business, it is counted as "money received by business"

A few pointers
- Cash is Real A/C
- Bank is Personal A/C

## Goods vs Assets
- Assets are economically benefecial to business
- Goods are things to be resold to someone else
  - When we buy goods, we keep them in Purchases A/C
  - When we sell goods, we keep them in Sales A/C

## Debtor and Creditor
- A debtor is a person or business that owes money to your company
- A creditor is a person or business that your company owes money to

## Journal Entry

- Original entry book. Transactions are recorded in a chronological / sequential order
- Special journals : Journal that records a specific type / category of transactions
- Structure
	- Date
	- Particular : The transaction
	- LF (Ledger Folio) : The page number of entry in ledger book
		- Journal : First source of transaction
		- Ledger : Classified into relevant accounts
		- Journal shows a sale, ledger shows that the sale happened in Sales Account
	- Dr Amount : Debited Amount
	- Cr Amount : Credited Amount

## Samples
The following table summarizes the accounting transactions discussed in the video, detailing the journal entry and the reasoning based on the Golden Rules of Accounting:

| Transaction | Journal Entry | Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Started business with capital | Cash A/c Dr. To Capital A/c | Cash is an asset coming into the business (Real A/c: Debit what comes in); Capital represents the owner's investment (Personal A/c: Credit the giver). |
| Purchased furniture for cash | Furniture A/c Dr. To Cash A/c | Furniture is an asset being acquired (Real A/c: Debit what comes in); Cash is an asset going out (Real A/c: Credit what goes out). |
| Paid Rent | Rent A/c Dr. To Cash A/c | Rent is an operating expense (Nominal A/c: Debit all expenses); Cash is an asset leaving the business (Real A/c: Credit what goes out). |
| Purchased goods via cheque | Purchase A/c Dr. To Bank A/c | Purchases are treated as expenses (Nominal A/c: Debit expenses); Bank is the giver of funds (Personal A/c: Credit the giver). |
| Received cash from a debtor | Bank A/c Dr. To Debtor A/c | The bank account increases as it receives funds (Personal A/c: Debit the receiver); The debtor is the giver of the payment (Personal A/c: Credit the giver). |
| Paid salary by cheque | Salary A/c Dr. To Bank A/c | Salary is an expense for the business (Nominal A/c: Debit expenses); The bank is the entity providing the payment (Personal A/c: Credit the giver). |
| Received interest | Cash A/c Dr. To Interest Received A/c | Cash is an asset coming in (Real A/c: Debit what comes in); Interest received is an income (Nominal A/c: Credit all incomes/gains). |
| Received cash from Amit | Cash A/c Dr. To Amit A/c | Cash is an asset coming in (Real A/c: Debit what comes in); Amit is the person paying the debt (Personal A/c: Credit the giver). |
| Paid cash to Sujeet | Sujeet A/c Dr. To Cash A/c | Sujeet is the receiver of the payment (Personal A/c: Debit the receiver); Cash is an asset going out (Real A/c: Credit what goes out). |
