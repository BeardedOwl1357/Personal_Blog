# Financial Statements: Stock vs. Flow

## Definition / Formula

Balance Sheet (Stock) | P&L & Cash Flow (Flow)

## Explanation

Businesses track their financial activities using three primary statements, which are classified as either 'Stock' or 'Flow' concepts depending on whether they measure data at a single point in time or over an interval.

A stock concept measures financial position at a single, specific point in time. The Balance Sheet is a stock concept, tracking assets and funding at a precise moment. A flow concept measures financial activity over a period or time interval. The Profit & Loss Statement (which tracks performance/profitability) and the Cash Flow Statement (which tracks cash movements) are both flow concepts.
