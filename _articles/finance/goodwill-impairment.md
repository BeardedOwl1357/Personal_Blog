# Goodwill Impairment

## Definition / Formula

Impairment = Asset Write-down

## Explanation

While goodwill is recorded during an acquisition, there is no guarantee that its value will remain intact forever. When the acquired brand, customer loyalty, or business synergies lose their economic value over time, the goodwill is considered 'impaired.'

When a goodwill impairment occurs, the business must write down the asset's value. This write-down causes two simultaneous adjustments: (1) On the Income Statement (Profit & Loss), a 'goodwill impairment loss' is reported as an operating expense, reducing net income. (2) On the Balance Sheet, the value of intangible assets decreases, and the company's Retained Earnings (under the equity section) drop by the exact same amount.
