# Customer Acquisition Cost

## Definition

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) is the average amount
a company spends to acquire one new customer.

## Formula

$$
CAC = \frac{Sales\ \&\ Marketing\ Cost}{New\ Customers}
$$

## Explanation

CAC is useful for understanding how efficiently
a company acquires customers.

For example, if a company spends ?10 lakh on sales
and marketing and acquires 1,000 customers:

$$
CAC = \frac{?10,00,000}{1,000} = ?1,000
$$

So the company spends an average of ?1,000 to acquire
each customer.
