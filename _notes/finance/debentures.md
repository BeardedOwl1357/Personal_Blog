# Debentures

## Definition / Formula

A formal **"I.O.U." note** issued by a private / public companies. Instead of bank giving them a loan, normal people can buy debentures for money

## Explanation

- Customer buy debentures for a term (5 years). Suppose a debenture of 10k
- Company promised to pay amount (10k) in 5 years and every year, pays interest
- Note that secured debentures are better than unsecured because they protect customers if a company goes bankrupt
