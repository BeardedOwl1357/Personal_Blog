# Bonds

## Definition / Formula

A big ass debenture, usually by mega-corporations and govts. Extremely safe unlike debentures. Lower interest rates

## Explanation

A debenture is a formal **"I.O.U." note** issued by a private / public companies. Instead of bank giving them a loan, normal people can buy debentures for money. 

Bonds are just extremely big debentures
