# Retained Earnings

## Definition / Formula

Retained Earnings = Cumulative Profits - Dividends

## Explanation

Retained earnings represent the accumulated profits of a business that have not been distributed to shareholders as dividends. Instead, this capital is kept by the firm to be reinvested into its core operations.

Retained earnings are reported under the equity section of the Balance Sheet. They are used for purchasing new assets, funding research and development, paying down debt, or general business expansion. Over time, healthy retained earnings signify a self-sustaining and growing business.
