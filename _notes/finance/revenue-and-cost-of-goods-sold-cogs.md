# Revenue and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

## Definition / Formula

Revenue/Profit = Unit Value (Selling Price) - COGS (Cost Price)

## Explanation

To make money, a business must sell its offerings at a price that exceeds the cost incurred to produce them. The cost of manufacturing or procuring these items is known as the Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).

The revenue or profit generated from each sale is determined by subtracting the COGS (cost price) from the unit value (selling price). Maximizing profitability requires either increasing the unit selling price or lowering the cost of goods sold without sacrificing quality.
