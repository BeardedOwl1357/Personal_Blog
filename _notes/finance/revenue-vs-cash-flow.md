# Revenue vs. Cash Flow

## Definition / Formula

Revenue (Accrued Sale) != Cash (Physical Money)

## Explanation

In business, making a sale does not always mean receiving immediate cash. While businesses prefer prompt cash payments, many offer goods or services on credit (sell first, collect later) to boost sales volume. A transaction is counted as a successful sale and recorded as revenue as soon as the credit sale occurs.

This creates a key distinction between revenue and cash. You can increase revenue by making a sale on credit today; however, the actual cash on hand will not increase until the customer pays the invoice. This delay is why a highly profitable business on paper can still face cash shortages if it manages its receivables poorly.
