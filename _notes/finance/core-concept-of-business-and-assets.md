# Core Concept of Business & Assets

## Definition / Formula

Assets: Resources that help us earn money

## Explanation

A business fundamentally exists to earn money. To achieve this, it must offer a product, service, or value proposition that others are willing to purchase.

To produce or create this offering, certain resources are required. The resources that help a business generate income and earn money are called assets. Therefore, assets are the engine of a business's revenue generation.
