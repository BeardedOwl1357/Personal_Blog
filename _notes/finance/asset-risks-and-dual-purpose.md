# Asset Risks & Dual Purpose

## Definition / Formula

Dual Purpose: Income Generation & Risk Management

## Explanation

While assets are designed to earn money, they also carry inherent trade-offs in the form of risks. These risks include physical damage (e.g., equipment breaking down) or the uncertainty of future benefits (such as purchasing a fleet of cars for a taxi business just before a global pandemic restricts travel).

To balance these risks, assets serve a dual purpose. They are categorized either as income generation assets (which actively help create revenue) or risk management assets (which reduce the risk or volatility of creating that revenue).
