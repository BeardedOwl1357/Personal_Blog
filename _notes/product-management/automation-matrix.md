# Automation Matrix

## Definition / Formula

While deciding what we should automate or not, we can follow a 2x2 matrix as a guideline

## Explanation

A combination of repeatbility and sensitivity defines whether we should automate something or not
- Repeatability : How repetitive something is
- Sensitivity : How much human involement is necessary

Based on their intensity
- High Repeatibility, Low Sensitivty = Fully automate
- High Repeatibility, High Sensitivty = Automate + human in the loop
- Low Repeatibility, High Sensitivty = Human led only
- Low Repeatibility, Low Sensitivty = Ignore
